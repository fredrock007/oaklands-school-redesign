SCHOOL UNIFORM

What learners need.

The four lists below are transcribed from the school’s recovered Uniform page. Check with the school before purchasing, as requirements may have changed.

SUMMER / GIRLS

Girls’ Summer Uniform

  • Summer school dress
  • Navy cardigan
  • White socks
  • Navy blue hair accessories
  • Navy blue scarves
SUMMER / BOYS

Boys’ Summer Uniform

  • Grey pants or shorts
  • White shirt
  • School tie
  • Black school shoes
  • Grey socks
WINTER / GIRLS

Girls’ Winter Uniform

  • Navy blue skirt (knee length)
  • School tie
  • Black school shoes
  • White socks
  • Navy jersey
  • Navy blue hair accessories
  • Navy blue Oaklands blazer
WINTER / BOYS

Boys’ Winter Uniform

  • Grey pants
  • White shirt
  • School tie
  • Black school shoes
  • Grey socks
  • Navy blue Oaklands blazer
